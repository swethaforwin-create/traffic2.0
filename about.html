🚦 About Dynamic Traffic AI

🤖 Our System

Dynamic Traffic AI is an intelligent traffic management prototype based on Autonomous Agentic AI.

🚦 Signal Optimization
Dynamically adjusts signal timing based on traffic conditions.
🔀 Traffic Diversion
Identifies alternative routes to reduce congestion.
🚑 Emergency Priority
Provides priority support for emergency vehicles.
🤖 Agent Coordination
Multiple AI agents communicate and coordinate traffic decisions.
📊 Traffic Dashboard
Provides an overview of the traffic management system.

🎯 Goal

To reduce traffic congestion, improve traffic flow and support faster emergency response using intelligent AI-based coordination.
